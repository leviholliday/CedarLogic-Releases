# CedarLogic downloads

**CedarLogic** is a free, open-source digital logic simulator. Drag in gates, wire them up and watch the signals flow; then press one key for the truth table, the Karnaugh map and the simplest formula.

**Opening it on a Mac the first time:** CedarLogic isn't distributed through the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once. If it says it can't check the app, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to CedarLogic. After that, updates arrive inside the app (CedarLogic › Check for Updates…), each with a short tour of what's new.

## What's in CedarLogic for Mac

- **Build fast.** 100+ parts (gates, flip-flops, registers, counters, multiplexers, decoders, adders, RAM, ROM). Press **A** and type to add one; drag pin to pin to wire; **C** connects while you drag.
- **Check your work.** **T** makes the truth table, a Karnaugh map for each light, and the simplest sum of products and product of sums. **Build from Formula** goes the other way.
- **See it run.** Simulation View runs the signal along every lit wire; the oscilloscope exports timing diagrams as PNG or PDF.
- **It keeps itself.** Every circuit lives in Your Circuits and saves itself, with version history. Templates (a lab page with your name on it, a counter, a 7-segment starter) and your own reusable parts.
- **Hand it in.** Export PNG or PDF with your name and whether it works, print, or save a .cdl for older CedarLogic. It opens classic .cdl circuits.

## About

CedarLogic began at [Cedarville University](https://www.cedarville.edu/) and was modernized and brought to the Mac by [Kieran Klukas](https://github.com/taciturnaxolotl/CedarLogic). This version was rebuilt and extended with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. Free software under the GPL-3.0; the source is at [leviholliday/CedarLogic](https://github.com/leviholliday/CedarLogic).
